None. T0.1 originally extracted the `.d.ts` parser into `scripts/lib/declared-exports.mjs`, which
created two exports whose only consumer was a test — pillar (a) FAIL, correctly caught by the Phase 0
mini review. Rather than defer a non-negotiable pillar or write a caller to satisfy it, the extraction
was **undone**: the parser has exactly ONE consumer today and lives with it.

`system-design-guardrails.md § G12` says extract on the repetition, not in anticipation of one. The
second consumer is T4.1's layer-invention gate, and extraction belongs to that task. The two existing
`.d.ts` parsers (`check-surface-parity.mjs`, `check-sandbox-parity.mjs`) are not repetitions of this
one — they answer a SUBPATH-scoped question while this answers a package-scoped one, and merging them
is the accidental coupling the same rule forbids.
