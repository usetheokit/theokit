Two Vite majors, two esbuild binaries — J9's TheoKit application, 2026-08-20

  npm ls esbuild --all
    ├─┬ @tailwindcss/vite@4.3.3
    │ └─┬ vite@7.3.6
    │   └── esbuild@0.28.2
    └─┬ theokit@0.48.14
      ├─┬ tsx@4.23.12
      │ └── esbuild@0.28.2 deduped
      └─┬ vite@6.4.3
        └── esbuild@0.25.12

  on disk:
    node_modules/esbuild                    0.28.2   12 MB
    node_modules/theokit/node_modules/esbuild  0.25.12  11 MB

  where it comes from:
    packages/theo/package.json                  "vite": "^6.4.3"
    create-theokit default template             "@tailwindcss/vite": "^4.0.0"  -> vite 7

`esbuild` runs a `postinstall` that fetches a platform binary. Two majors means
two fetches and 23 MB, not one. This is NOT the inherent cost of choosing Vite,
which the programme's scope endorses ("Escrever bundler proprio (Vite/Rollup
resolvem)" is out of scope) — it is a version skew between what the framework
pins and what its own scaffold pulls in through Tailwind.

Not measured: how much of the 19.8 s of script time the second copy accounts
for. The two are the same order of size, so the naive read is roughly half of
esbuild's share, but that is arithmetic and not a measurement, and it is written
here as arithmetic.
